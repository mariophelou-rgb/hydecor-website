About HyDecor · Since 1918

Four generations.One family.One craft.

A Lebanese family atelier of statues, sculptures and architectural décor, carried from Armenia to Lebanon and on through four generations.

Our story

Built on craftsmanship, creativity and family

  1. Our story began with our great-grandmother, who came to Lebanon from Armenia and began creating statues by hand.

  2. Our great-grandfather, a talented painter, worked alongside her, bringing each piece to life.

  3. 1918

    The family business has been working since 1918.

  4. The craft was passed to our grandfather, then to our father, and today it has reached the fourth generation.

  5. I now carry the family business forward, continuing a tradition built on craftsmanship, creativity, family, and generations of dedication.

To begin

Bring us what you have

A conversation can start from very little. Share whatever you have, and we will talk it through with you.

  • A concept

    A few words on the piece, the setting or the feeling you want.

  • A reference image

    A photograph of a sculpture, a detail or a piece from our collection.

  • A drawing

    A sketch, an elevation or an architect’s plan.

  • Dimensions

    The height, width or space the piece must fit.

  • Project requirements

    Quantities, the type of space and any finish you have in mind.

How an inquiry works

From first message to finished piece

This is how we suggest working together. Specifications, finishes, timing and delivery are discussed and agreed with you for each piece.

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Send your concept, references, drawings or dimensions on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

  2. 02

    Discuss requirements

    We talk through the setting, size, quantity and the look you want.

  3. 03

    Confirm the piece

    We agree the proposed piece and its specifications with you.

  4. 04

    Production and finishing

    We discuss how the piece will be made and finished.

  5. 05

    Delivery or installation

    We arrange delivery, or installation where it is offered for your project.
